Find Powerful Relics
  • Tasked by Kaelthar in Session 25 before the party left Stonegrip
  • Find powerful magical artifacts hidden across Gal'Dor to help fight the demonic incursion pushing out of Sparrowight against Stonegrip Citadel
  • Primary lead: the Instruments of the First Oath — five ancient relics tied to the fate of the world
    • The Caldera Beads — monks turned prayers into beads; held at Caldera. May have been taken by the demons who invaded (see Caldera quest below)
    • The Arkanhold Relic — a Hooded Watcher is said to guard something glowing in Arkanhold
    • The Mask — worn by a man who spat in the face of a god and lived
    • The Bridge — forged by a dwarf with molten hands who raised and shattered a bridge of fire
    • The Mantle — worn by a queen who walked into battle draped in roses
Stop the Demons at Caldera Monastery
  • Demons have invaded Caldera Monastery — connected to Amanthi's unresolved demonic ties
  • The Grand Master was heard screaming Amanthi's name during the attack — his fate is unknown
  • In Amanthi's dream (Session 28), the two demons walked into Caldera with glowing beads draped across their shoulders — the demons and the Caldera Beads may be one and the same lead
  • Reach the Portal District in Vayehelm to portal to Caldera
    • Party is currently split — Gnarl and Amanthi heading for the Portal District; others in hiding
    • Portals in Vayehelm are "not for travellers — they're for problems that can't wait"
